Common questions

My weight or body data from my smart scale isn't showing up.
In your scale's app (for example Withings), turn on Apple Health sync. Then on your iPhone go to Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Momentum and turn on all categories. New readings appear when you open Momentum.
The screen turns off during workouts.
Momentum keeps the screen on while it's open. Check Settings → Coach voice & workouts → Keep screen on.
My Apple Watch workout didn't show on my phone.
Open Momentum on both. The Watch resends finished workouts until the phone confirms them, and the phone also recovers them from Apple Health after about 10 minutes.
How do I delete my data?
Settings → Backup, privacy & data → Delete all my data.
How do I turn off the AI coach?
Settings → Coach voice & workouts → AI coach.
