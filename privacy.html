Privacy Policy

Momentum: AI Fitness Coach · Last updated October 21, 2026

The short version: your data stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Momentum has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and never sells your data. The only time data leaves your devices for an outside company is when you use the AI coach, and only after you agree.

What Momentum stores, and where

  • On your iPhone and Apple Watch: your profile (name, age, sex, height and weight, goals, equipment), your health-check answers, your training plan and levels, workout history (including GPS routes), body check-ins and measurements, progress photos, pain notes and your coach chat. Progress photos never leave your phone.
  • Your own iCloud backup: your plan, history, settings and recent coach chat are backed up to your private iCloud storage so a new phone can restore them. Progress photos are not backed up. Only you can access your iCloud; we can't.

Apple Health

With your permission, Momentum reads data from Apple Health (such as steps, heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, VO₂ max, workouts, and body measurements like weight, BMI and body fat from devices such as smart scales) to personalize your plan and show your progress. It writes your workouts and body check-ins back to Apple Health. Health data is used only to provide Momentum's features. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone except as described for the AI coach below. You can change Momentum's Health access at any time in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Location and motion

During walks, runs, rides and races, Momentum uses GPS and motion sensors to measure distance, pace, steps, cadence and elevation. Location is used only during workouts you start, and routes are stored on your device (and in Apple Health when saved there).

The AI coach (Anthropic)

Momentum's coach is powered by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, PBC. Before the coach is used for the first time, Momentum asks for your permission. If you agree, then when you message the coach or finish a workout (for a short debrief), Momentum sends Anthropic the information the coach needs to reply: your first name, age, sex, weight, goals, equipment, health-check answers, plan and recent workouts, a summary of your Apple Health data, any pain you've logged, and your messages. Progress photos and exact GPS routes are never sent.

The data is sent securely, used only to generate the coach's reply, and handled under Anthropic's commercial terms: Anthropic does not use it to train its models and keeps it only for a limited time. See Anthropic's privacy policy. You can turn the AI coach off at any time in Settings → Coach voice & workouts; the rest of Momentum keeps working.

Workout partner

If you pair with a workout partner, Momentum shares a small status through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit): your first name, how many workouts you've done this week, your planned number, your streak, and the name and date of your last workout. It's stored under a random pairing code and is visible only to someone who has that code. Unpairing or deleting your data removes it.

Train together

Train together connects directly to phones nearby over Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi to share your first name, the workout and a shared start time. Nothing goes over the internet or to us.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • We never sell or rent your data.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can export your data or delete all of it at any time in Settings → Backup, privacy & data → Delete all my data. This erases everything Momentum stores on your phone, your iCloud backup and your partner status. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone.

Children

Momentum is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Not medical advice

Momentum provides general fitness guidance and is not a medical device or a substitute for professional medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise program.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for meaningful changes, let you know in the app. Questions or requests: momentum.ai.help@gmail.com.
